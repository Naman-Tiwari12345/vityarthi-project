# Project Statement

## Problem Statement
Students often spend money on food, transportation, entertainment and other daily activities without maintaining an organized record. This makes it difficult to understand spending patterns and control monthly expenses.

## Scope
The project provides a simple Python-based system for recording expenses, managing a monthly budget and generating basic spending analytics.

## Target Users
College and university students who want to track their daily expenses.

## High-Level Features
1. User registration and login
2. Expense management
3. Budget management
4. Expense reporting and analytics
5. Data storage using JSON
